Hard limit for Opal credits

What changes for admins and for people using Opal when an organization can stop Opal at its credit limit. Built from the existing Credits prototype and the research on how other AI platforms enforce limits.

In the prototype today Suggested by research, needs a decision Blocked state

Who does what

The admin owns the limit. Everyone else feels it when Opal stops. Dashed items are not built yet.

Enforcement flow

Every chat, agent and scheduled workflow passes one check before it runs. Turning the limit off is always available, so an admin can never be locked out of continuing.

What the screens look like

Three states, two audiences. Admins get the control and the warnings. Users get a clear reason when Opal stops, and a way to ask for more.

Decisions for the team

These come from the research and are not in the prototype yet.

  • Allow a temporary override?Admin raises the limit with a written reason and an audit entry, without turning enforcement off.
  • Auto-resume by default?Opt-in resume of blocked workflows after a reset or a raise, with a visible "will resume" status.
  • Department limits now or later?V1 budgets are soft. Enforcing per department, with extensions, would be a separate phase.
  • How to handle the data lag?Usage arrives about 2 hours late, so Opal can pass the limit before it stops. Options: pre-check large actions, or a small safety buffer.
